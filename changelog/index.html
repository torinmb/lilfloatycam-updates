Lil Floaty Cam Changelog

1.9 October 19, 2026

  • Resize handles are now thin arcs on the corners that appear at the corner nearest your cursor
  • Four corner radius dots slide along the edge from square to circle to square, and ease onto the circle in the middle
  • Corner radius stays proportional when you resize the camera, so a circle stays a circle
  • Smoother corner radius dragging

1.8 October 19, 2026

  • With Remove Background on, only you catch clicks and drags; clicks outside your cutout pass through to the apps underneath
  • Resize and corner radius grips appear when you hover the cutout (hidden in Streaming Mode)

1.7 October 19, 2026

  • The app now shows in the Dock. Click its icon to open the controls (turn this off in Settings → Advanced)
  • Resize grips and the corner radius dot are easier to see on any background

1.6 October 19, 2026

  • Tooltips on the resize grips, corner radius dot and hover toolbar buttons
  • Menu switches now show your accent color when on, including on macOS 26

1.5 October 19, 2026

  • Removed the ripple when the cursor first moves over the camera; click and edge-bounce ripples are unchanged

1.4 October 19, 2026

  • Choose the camera's aspect ratio right from the menu, just below Camera

1.3 October 19, 2026

  • Updates now pop up when one is available, and the app checks every time it launches

1.2 October 19, 2026

  • Drag any corner of the camera to resize it, and drag the dot on the top edge to change the corner radius
  • Reorganized menu with icons, plus an Auto-Check for Updates toggle
  • Full screen now covers the Dock and menu bar, and is click-through with a transparent background
  • Larger, easier-to-grab border that stays visible while you drag

1.1 October 19, 2026

  • Added the app version to Settings so you can confirm an update installed

1.0 October 19, 2026

  • First release, with automatic updates
